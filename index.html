About me

Words + pixels + code

I turn a brief into a finished paper, a clean design or a working website.

I hold a BSc in Information Technology from Northwestern Polytechnic University (2022). Since 2023 I have written academic work for clients on online freelance platforms, researching each topic, structuring the argument and citing sources in the style the client needs.

From 2023 to 2025 I also worked in IT support at a digital cyber cafe, solving technical problems for customers every day. That work taught me to stay calm, explain things plainly and finish what I start.

Alongside writing, I design in Photoshop, Illustrator and Canva, and I build responsive websites. Clients can get the paper and the visuals from one person.
